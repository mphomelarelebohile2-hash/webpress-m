Who We Are

About PressWave Media

Our Mission

PressWave Media was founded on a single conviction: that truthful, independent journalism is the cornerstone of a functioning democracy. Since our launch in 2018, we have grown from a small newsroom in Maseru to a multi-platform media organisation reaching audiences across Southern Africa and beyond.

What We Do

We cover politics, economics, culture, and technology through investigative reports, live broadcasts, podcasts, and digital features. Our team of over 30 journalists, photographers, and editors works around the clock to ensure our audience receives accurate, timely, and contextual reporting on the stories that matter most.

Our Values

Accuracy, fairness, and editorial independence are non-negotiable at PressWave. We hold power to account, amplify marginalised voices, and foster public discourse through rigorous and ethical journalism. Every story we publish reflects our commitment to serving the public interest above all else.
